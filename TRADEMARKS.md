# The SwitchBoard-AV™ name and logo

The SwitchBoard-AV **software** is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later
([LICENSE](LICENSE)), with the additional terms in [NOTICE](NOTICE). That license lets you copy, change and share
the code.

The **name "SwitchBoard-AV" and the SwitchBoard-AV logo** (`public/logo-default.png`) are different: they tell
people which project something comes from, and they belong to Brett Cupp. The license does **not** give you the
right to use them as your own brand (this is the trademark term in [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The rules below say what
that means in practice. The aim is simple: people should be able to tell the original from a copy that has
been changed.

## You may

- **Run the unmodified software** and keep its name and logo. A school or office running SwitchBoard-AV as it
  comes is simply using SwitchBoard-AV.
- **Share unmodified copies** of the project with its name and logo as they are.
- **Refer to the project by name, truthfully**: "based on SwitchBoard-AV", "compatible with SwitchBoard-AV",
  "works with SwitchBoard-AV", "powered by SwitchBoard-AV". Articles, reviews and tutorials are welcome.
- **Put your own organization's name, logo and colours on your own install** with the Branding settings. That
  changes what your staff see; it does not turn your install into a different project. The fixed "Powered by
  SwitchBoard-AV" wording stays, because it is true.

## You may not

- **Call a modified version "SwitchBoard-AV"**, or use the logo for it. A modified version is one where the code
  itself has been changed, not one where only the Branding settings were used.
- **Use the name or logo, or anything easily mistaken for them**, for another product, service, company, domain
  name or social media account.
- **Suggest that the project made, approved or supports** your version, product or service.

## If you publish a modified version

1. Give it **its own name and its own logo**.
2. Keep the credit **"SwitchBoard-AV by Brett Cupp"** where [NOTICE](NOTICE) says (you may introduce it as
   "Based on SwitchBoard-AV by Brett Cupp" and add your own credits).
3. Replace the fixed "Powered by SwitchBoard-AV" wording on the sign-in page and main page with
   "Based on SwitchBoard-AV". That wording says the software is the original, and a changed version is not.

## Questions, or something else in mind

If you want to use the name or logo in a way that is not covered here, open an issue and ask. Reasonable
requests are usually fine; the point is that people are not misled.

SwitchBoard-AV™ is claimed as a trademark of Brett Cupp. The ™ symbol only says the name is claimed as a
trademark; it does not mean it is registered.
